Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

LinkWithBuyers ("we", "us") provides personalized video outreach and email campaign services to businesses. This policy explains what information we handle, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we handle

  • Account information you give us: your name, business name, email address, phone number, and LinkedIn profile, which you submit through our forms or provide directly.
  • Email sending authorization: with your explicit consent, you may connect your Gmail or Microsoft 365 mailbox through Google's or Microsoft's official sign-in flow. We receive a limited authorization token that permits sending campaign emails on your behalf through the gmail.send scope (Gmail) or the Mail.Send Microsoft Graph permission (Microsoft 365). We never see or store your email password.
  • Campaign data: the names, email addresses, and profile URLs of the contacts you ask us to reach, and the delivery/watch status of the messages sent.
  • Campaign briefs: descriptions of your business, audience, and goals that you submit so we can write your email sequence.
  • LinkedIn credentials: if you ask us to send LinkedIn messages on your behalf and choose to provide your LinkedIn login, we store it encrypted. You may revoke it at any time by contacting us.

How we use information

  • To send the email campaigns and outreach messages you configure.
  • To write and improve your campaign content.
  • To show you reporting on delivery and engagement.
  • To honor unsubscribe and opt-out requests immediately.

What we never do

  • We do not read your mailbox contents. Our Gmail authorization is limited to the gmail.send scope (sending only); we cannot read, search, or download your email.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information or your contacts' information with third parties for their own marketing.
  • We do not send email from your connected mailbox except for campaigns you approved.

Data retention and deletion

Campaign and contact data is retained while your account is active. When you end service, we delete contact data and revoke stored authorizations within 30 days upon request. You can revoke Gmail access at any time from your Google Account permissions page, and Microsoft access from your Microsoft account privacy settings.

Contacts

If a contact of one of our clients wants to unsubscribe, they can reply "unsubscribe" to any campaign email; replies are honored immediately. For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact David Zopf at david@linkwithbuyers.com.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify clients directly.
